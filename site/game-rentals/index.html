Getting Started with June Digitals

Everything you need to know to Get Started
How do I rent a digital game?

Pick your game → log into the provided June Digitals account on your PS4/PS5 → download via WiFi or mobile data → PLAY!

How do I rent a physical disc game?

Subscribe to our unlimited exchange monthly plan → fill up the subscription form https://m.me/junedigitalaccess → install using our original disc → play until ready to exchange.

What's the difference between digital and physical rentals?

Digital = instant access via shared account, no swapping.
Physical = original discs, unlimited exchanges under a monthly subscription.

Can I exchange my physical disc anytime?

Yes — swap for a new title whenever you're ready, as long as your subscription is active.
