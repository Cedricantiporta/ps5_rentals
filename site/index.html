What's the difference between digital and physical rentals?

Digital = instant access via shared account, no swapping.
Physical = original discs, unlimited exchanges under a monthly subscription.

Can I exchange my physical disc anytime?

Yes — swap for a new title whenever you're ready, as long as your subscription is active.
