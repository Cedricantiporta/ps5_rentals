What you’re renting

How it works

  1. We send your login. Your PS5 account details come through Messenger. Sign in on your console with them.
  2. Download the game. Set our account as your Primary PS5 so the game plays on your own profile.
  3. Play for your whole rental period. Your countdown is on your rentals page — no surprises.
  4. We handle the rest. When time’s up the rental closes automatically.

Bored? Swap it

  1. Open My Rentals (button below — your ID is already in).
  2. Tap the game you’re playing, then tap Swap.
  3. Pick any available game and confirm.
  4. We approve it and send the new login. Your end date doesn’t change and swapping is free.

You’ll see “Waiting for approval” on your rentals page until we confirm it. We hold the game for you in the meantime.

The rules — short version

  • DO keep the account details to yourself.
  • DON’T change the password or email. It locks you out too, and ends the rental.
  • DON’T share or resell the account.
  • DO message us the moment anything looks wrong — we’ll sort it out.
