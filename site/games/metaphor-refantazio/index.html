Trophy vs Non-Trophy

Trophy: play on your own PSN profile so trophies and saves stay with you. Non-Trophy: play on the rented game profile. Both provide access to the full game, subject to live slot availability.

How the rental works

  1. Open the live game picker and choose Weekly or Monthly.
  2. Choose Trophy or Non-Trophy.
  3. Send the prepared request to June Digitals on Messenger.
  4. Wait for staff confirmation before payment or account setup.

Security deposit and availability

A refundable ₱150 security deposit may apply to a new rental. Live availability can change while requests are being processed, so a slot is final only after June Digitals confirms it.
